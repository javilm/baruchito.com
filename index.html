Hello, dear visitor!

If you've found your way here, you probably remember us. We're Baru, Julia, Flan, Nana, Oscar, Luna, and Cleo — a family of hamsters who lived in Tokyo, Japan between 2003 and 2008.

Our human, Javi, set up this blog so we could share our daily adventures with the world. We wrote about napping in tissue-paper nests, stuffing our cheeks with sunflower seeds, racing on our wheels at 3 a.m., and all the other very important things that hamsters do.

Baru was the first to arrive, a brave little golden boy. Julia came next — she was sweet and gentle. Then came Flan, who fell in love with Baru, and together they had five babies: Nana, Coco, Poteto, Haru, and Hana. Most of the little ones went to live with other families, but Nana stayed with us. Oscar was our one Djungarian hamster — tiny, round, and full of mischief. Luna and Cleo joined the family later and kept the tradition going until 2008.

We've all crossed the Rainbow Bridge now, but we had wonderful lives full of treats, warm beds, and a human who loved us very much. This blog was our little corner of the internet, and even though we can't post anymore, we're happy that people still remember us.
